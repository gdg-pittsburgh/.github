# ⚫🟡 GDG Pittsburgh

### Google Developer Group — Pittsburgh

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for the Pittsburgh chapter of
Google Developer Groups (GDG).

We’re building a community of developers, founders, researchers, students, creators, and technologists focused on learning, collaboration, and real-world problem solving using modern Google technologies.

---

## What We Do

GDG Pittsburgh hosts:

* Technical workshops
* AI & Machine Learning sessions
* Google Cloud Platform training
* Gemini & Agent Development workshops
* Speaker series with local and national experts
* Hackathons and collaborative projects
* Networking events across Pittsburgh’s growing tech ecosystem

We believe in:

> Adding value. Solving problems. Making introductions.

---

## 💡 Focus Areas

* Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
* Gemini & Google AI
